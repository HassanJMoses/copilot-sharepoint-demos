Policyholder Notice

This notice applies to {{business_name}}'s coverage under policy {{policy_number}} in the State of Louisiana. It bundles several notices Contoso Insurance is required, or chooses, to provide alongside your policy. Keep it with your policy documents.

Keeping your information current

Please notify Contoso promptly if your business address, mailing address, or ownership changes. A delay in updating your information could affect how quickly we can reach you about your policy or a claim.

Filing a complaint

If you have a concern about your policy that your agent cannot resolve, you may contact your state's department of insurance. Contact information is available on your state's official insurance department website. Please have your policy number, {{policy_number}}, available when you call.

Cancellation and nonrenewal

Either party may cancel this policy with written notice as described in the General Conditions section of your policy. We will give you advance written notice before nonrenewing or materially changing your coverage at renewal, as required by law.

Fraud warning statement

Any person who knowingly presents a false or fraudulent claim for payment of a loss, or who knowingly presents false information in an application for insurance, may be guilty of a crime and may be subject to fines, confinement in prison, or denial of insurance benefits.

Economic and trade sanctions notice

This policy does not provide coverage, and we will not make any payment, to the extent that doing so would violate applicable trade or economic sanctions laws, including those administered by the U.S. Department of the Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control.

Disclosure of premium and coverage for certified acts of terrorism

Coverage for losses caused by certified acts of terrorism under applicable federal law is included in this policy. You should know that, effective the date shown in your Declarations, any losses resulting from certified acts of terrorism may be partially reimbursed by the United States government under a formula established by federal law. The portion of your premium attributable to this coverage, if any, is shown on your Coverage Summary or Declarations.

Guaranty association notice

Contoso Insurance is a member of the Louisiana Insurance Guaranty Association. If we become insolvent, the Association may pay some or all of your covered claim, subject to the limits and exclusions in the Louisiana Revised Statutes. This notice is informational only and does not form part of your policy.

Electronic delivery of documents

If you consented to electronic delivery on your application, policy documents, notices, and billing statements will be delivered to the email address on file instead of by mail, unless you later withdraw that consent in writing.

Dispute resolution

Before filing a lawsuit over a coverage dispute, you agree to first allow us a reasonable opportunity to investigate and respond to your concern through our internal appeals process, as described in the General Conditions of your policy.

Where to find more information

This notice summarizes several separate disclosures for convenience. The full text of each underlying requirement is available on request, and your policy's General Conditions govern wherever this summary and the policy differ.
