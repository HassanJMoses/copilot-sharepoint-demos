Rating factors

Your premium for commercial vehicle coverage reflects each vehicle's year, make, model, use class, and radius of operation, along with your business type and loss history. A change in any of these factors, including adding a vehicle used for a materially different purpose, may change your premium.

Driver requirements

Only drivers who hold a valid license appropriate for the vehicle they operate, and who are not excluded by endorsement, are covered while driving a vehicle on this schedule. You are responsible for verifying that drivers meet this requirement before assigning them to a covered vehicle.

Temporary substitute vehicles

If a covered vehicle is out of service for repair, servicing, or loss, a temporary substitute vehicle of similar type is automatically covered at the same limits, for up to 30 days, provided it is not owned by you.
