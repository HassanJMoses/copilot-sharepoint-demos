Notice to Employees -- Workers' Compensation

THIS NOTICE MUST BE POSTED WHERE EMPLOYEES CAN SEE IT

 

{{business_name}} carries workers' compensation insurance through Contoso Insurance, policy {{policy_number}}.

If you are injured on the job, you have the right to:

  • Receive medical care for a work-related injury or illness, with no cost to you for reasonably necessary treatment.
  • File a claim for lost wages if your injury keeps you from working.
  • Choose your treating doctor from your employer's approved network, where applicable, or request a change of doctor under the rules of your state's workers' compensation system.
  • Request a copy of your claim file and any medical reports relied on in a decision about your claim.
  • Appeal a decision about your claim through the dispute resolution process available in your state.
  • Return to work without retaliation for having filed a good-faith claim.
  • Have a representative, including an attorney, assist you with your claim.

Your employer's duties

  • Report a work injury to Contoso Insurance promptly after learning of it.
  • Post this notice where employees can see it and provide information about your rights on request.
  • Not take adverse action against you for filing a claim in good faith, including termination, demotion, or reduction in hours.
  • Maintain workers' compensation coverage for the duration of your employment, as required by law.

If you disagree with a decision about your claim

You may request a review of the decision through your state's workers' compensation agency. Many states offer an informal dispute resolution process before a formal hearing is required; your state agency's website explains the steps and any deadlines that apply.

Common questions

Will I be paid while I'm out of work? Temporary income benefits generally begin after a short waiting period and continue until you reach maximum medical improvement or return to work at your pre-injury wage, whichever comes first.

Can I be fired for filing a claim? No. Filing a good-faith workers' compensation claim is a protected activity, and retaliation for doing so is prohibited by law.

What if I disagree with my assigned doctor? In most states you may request a change of treating doctor, subject to the rules of the network or system your employer participates in.

For questions about your rights under workers' compensation, contact your state's workers' compensation agency or the Contoso claims line listed elsewhere in this packet.

A note for supervisors and managers

Supervisors should know where this posting is displayed, understand the basic injury-reporting process, and never discourage an employee from reporting an injury or seeking medical care. Delayed or discouraged reporting is one of the most common causes of complications in a workers' compensation claim.

Posting requirements

This notice must be displayed in a conspicuous place at each work location where covered employees can reasonably be expected to see it, such as a break room or near a time clock. If your business operates at more than one location, a copy must be posted at each one.

 

Language access

If a significant portion of your workforce is more comfortable reading a language other than English, you should post this notice in that language as well where required by your state; contact Contoso for a translated version if one is not already in your packet.

Record of posting

Keep a record of where and when this notice was posted at each location. If you replace or relocate this posting, update your record accordingly -- some states require this as part of a compliance audit.
