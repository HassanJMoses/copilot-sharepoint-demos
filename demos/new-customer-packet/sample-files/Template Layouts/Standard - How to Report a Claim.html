How to Report a Claim

If something happens, here's how to reach us and what to expect.

24-hour claims line

1-800-555-0100. Have your policy number, {{policy_number}}, ready when you call. You can also start a claim online at contosoinsurance.example/claims.

What to have ready

  • A description of what happened and when
  • Photos of any damage or injury, if it's safe to take them
  • Contact information for anyone else involved
  • A police or incident report, if one was filed
  • Your policy number, {{policy_number}}

The claims process, step by step

1. Report the claim by phone or online. We'll open a claim file and assign an adjuster.

2. An adjuster contacts you within one business day to confirm coverage and explain what documentation is needed.

3. We investigate, which may include reviewing documentation, inspecting damage, or speaking with witnesses.

4. We provide a coverage decision and, where applicable, an estimate of the amount payable.

5. Once you agree on the amount, or any dispute is resolved, we issue payment.

Guidance by type of claim

Liability claim: do not admit fault at the scene. Give the other party our claims contact information and let the adjuster handle communication with them.

Property claim: take reasonable steps to prevent further damage, such as covering a damaged roof, and keep receipts for any temporary repairs -- we may reimburse reasonable costs.

Commercial vehicle claim: exchange insurance information with the other driver, do not leave the scene before required, and request a police report where available.

Workers' compensation claim: get the injured employee medical care first, then report the injury to us; see the Workers' Compensation Notice of Coverage in this packet for benefit details.

If another party may be responsible

If a loss was caused by someone else, such as a vendor or another driver, we may pursue recovery from them on your behalf under your policy's subrogation condition. This does not change how quickly we handle your claim.

What happens next

A Contoso claims adjuster will reach out within one business day to confirm coverage and walk you through next steps. Most straightforward claims receive a coverage decision within two weeks; complex claims may take longer, and we'll keep you updated along the way.

Keeping your business running during a claim

If a covered loss disrupts your operations and you purchased the Business Income and Extra Expense extension under your Property Coverage Form, let your adjuster know right away -- documenting lost income and extra expenses as they happen makes that part of the claim faster to resolve.

After your claim closes

You'll receive a written summary of the claim outcome. If you disagree with a decision, contact your adjuster first; most disagreements are resolved informally. Your policy's appraisal condition is also available for property-value disputes, as described in your coverage form.

 

Large or catastrophic events

Following a widespread event such as a hurricane or major storm, response times may be longer than usual due to claim volume. We prioritize claims involving safety hazards and total losses, and will post event-specific updates and extended claims-line hours at contosoinsurance.example/claims when applicable.

Escalating a claim

If you've worked with your adjuster and still have concerns, you may ask to speak with a claims supervisor, or contact Contoso's customer advocacy line, available at the number in the footer of this document.
